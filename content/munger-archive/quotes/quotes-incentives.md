# 芒格论激励

13 条有出处的语录。[全部主题 →](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/quotes/)

“给我看激励机制，我就能告诉你结果。”

Poor Charlie's Almanack

“在该思考激励机制之威力的时候，千万、千万不要去想别的事情。”

The Psychology of Human Misjudgment (Poor Charlie's Almanack)[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/psychology-of-human-misjudgment-1995/)

“管理上最重要的一条规则，或许就是：‘把激励机制设对。’”

The Psychology of Human Misjudgment (Poor Charlie's Almanack) · as recalled[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/psychology-of-human-misjudgment-1995/)

出自《人类误判心理学》一文中联邦快递夜班的例子；不同版本的措辞略有出入。

“我想，在理解激励机制的威力这件事上，我这一辈子都处在同龄人中前5%的水平，可即便如此，我这一辈子都低估了它。没有哪一年不出点意外，把我认知的边界又往外推一点。”

The Psychology of Human Misjudgment (Poor Charlie's Almanack) · 1995[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/psychology-of-human-misjudgment-1995/)

最早是1995年一次演讲中讲的；公认的措辞出自经过扩充的《穷查理宝典》版本。

“我们想赢的是路亚钓鱼巡回赛，而养老基金顾问卖的是渔具。我们对卖渔具没兴趣，我们感兴趣的是把鱼钓上来。”

Whitney Tilson's notes, Wesco Financial annual meeting, Pasadena, May 7, 2003 (Q&A, 'Pension Fund Consultants') · 2003

他关于"干事的人对卖东西的人"的论点（他的原话是‘沃伦和我’）。

“我这辈子一直躲着薪酬顾问。我简直找不出词来表达我对他们的鄙夷。”

Berkshire Hathaway annual meeting, 2017 (Omaha) · 2017[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/berkshire-hathaway-annual-meetings/)

“那些设计出轻易就能被钻空子的制度的人，活该下到地狱最底层那一圈。”

"Academic Economics: Strengths and Faults After Considering Interdisciplinary Needs," Herb Kay Undergraduate Lecture, University of California, Santa Barbara, October 3, 2003 · 2003[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/ucsb-2003-academic-economics/)

“大自然的铁律是：你奖励什么，就得到什么。你要是想招蚂蚁，就往地上撒糖。”

Tren Griffin, 'Charlie Munger: The Complete Investor' (2015) — illustrating Munger's Reward-and-Punishment Superresponse Tendency · as recalled

出自格里芬的书；具体场合无法考据到某段录音。

“我问他，‘我的天，这些都是紫的绿的。鱼真会咬这种假饵？’他说，‘先生，我又不是卖给鱼的。’”

Charlie Munger, "A Lesson on Elementary, Worldly Wisdom As It Relates to Investment Management & Business" (the "Art of Stock Picking" talk), lecture to Prof. Guilford Babcock's students, USC Business School, 1994; published in Outstanding Investor Digest, May 5, 1995 · 1994[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/usc-1994-worldly-wisdom/)

他常讲的一个钓具笑话；妙就妙在售货员那句关于激励机制的点睛之笔。

“最后，有人想出个办法：付给这些人钱，不再按小时算，而是按一个班次算——活儿干完了，他们就都能回家。结果呢，他们的问题一夜之间就没了。”

Charlie Munger, "A Lesson on Elementary, Worldly Wisdom As It Relates to Investment Management & Business" (the "Art of Stock Picking" talk), USC Business School, 1994; published in Outstanding Investor Digest, May 5, 1995 · 1994[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/usc-1994-worldly-wisdom/)

他举的联邦快递夜间分拣的例子——按班次付钱，而不是按小时。

“在官僚机构里，你以为活儿从你的收件筐挪进别人的收件筐就算干完了。可当然不是。要等AT&T把该交付的东西真正交付出去，活儿才算干完。”

Charlie Munger, "A Lesson on Elementary, Worldly Wisdom As It Relates to Investment Management & Business" (the "Art of Stock Picking" talk), USC Business School, 1994; published in Outstanding Investor Digest, May 5, 1995 · 1994[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/usc-1994-worldly-wisdom/)

“这就是我说的‘冲着费用去的购买’，很多都是这样——人们买东西是为了赚那笔费用。”

Daily Journal Corporation annual meeting (February 12, 2020) · 2020[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/daily-journal-2020/)

谈私募股权／风险投资行业那套靠收费驱动的文化。

“可惜的是，‘讲理由倾向’实在太强，哪怕一个人给出的是毫无意义或干脆错误的理由，也照样能提高别人对他命令和请求的服从度。”

"The Psychology of Human Misjudgment" (revised essay), Poor Charlie's Almanack — Reason-Respecting Tendency (No. 24) (2005) · 2005[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/psychology-of-human-misjudgment-1995/)

他提出的"讲理由倾向"——哪怕理由毫无意义，也能提高服从度。
