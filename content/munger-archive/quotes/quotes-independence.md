Wesco Financial annual meeting, 2000 (Pasadena), per Whitney Tilson's contemporaneous notes (May 15, 2000) · 2000

Wesco Financial annual meeting, 2000 (Pasadena), per Whitney Tilson's contemporaneous notes (May 15, 2000) · 2000
