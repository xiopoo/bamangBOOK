"人生与事业上的许多成功，源于懂得自己该避开什么：早逝、糟糕的婚姻，诸如此类。"

Wesco Financial annual meeting, 2000 (Pasadena), per Whitney Tilson's notes · 2000 · as recalled

出自惠特尼·蒂尔森当时所做的笔记（部分为转述），故措辞为近似。

"就我个人而言，如今我已养成一种双轨分析法。第一，理性地看，真正左右相关利益的因素是什么？第二，潜意识层面的影响又是什么——大脑在潜意识中自动做着这些事，它们大体上有用，却常常出错。"

"A Lesson on Elementary, Worldly Wisdom," USC Business School (1994), reprinted in Poor Charlie's Almanack · 1994[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/usc-1994-worldly-wisdom/)

"你为什么要拿自己已经拥有、也确实需要的东西，去冒险换取自己既没有、也不需要的东西？真是蠢透了。"

Daily Journal Corporation annual meeting, 2018 · 2018[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/daily-journal-2018/)

谈保证金交易与杠杆——他在前面铺垫道：'当你已经富有时，那简直是疯了。'

"不惜一切代价远离疯狂。疯狂远比你以为的更常见。人很容易就滑进疯狂里。总之就是躲开它，躲开它，躲开它。"

CNBC, "Charlie Munger: A Life of Wit and Wisdom" (final interview with Becky Quick, taped Nov. 14, 2023; aired Nov. 30, 2023) · 2023[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/cnbc-final-interview-2023/)

"我躲开了那些惯常的失败方式。……我这辈子的策略，从来都是避开一切惯常的失败方式。你教我打牌的错误打法，我就会把它避开。"

CNBC, "Charlie Munger: A Life of Wit and Wisdom" (final interview with Becky Quick, taped Nov. 14, 2023; aired Nov. 30, 2023) · 2023[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/cnbc-final-interview-2023/)

Charlie Munger, Harvard School (Los Angeles) commencement address, "How to Guarantee a Life of Misery," June 13, 1986 (Poor Charlie's Almanack, Talk One) · 1986 · as recalled[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/harvard-1986-misery/)

"如果你只用一两个[模型]，那么人类心理的本性会让你扭曲现实去迁就你的模型，或者至少你会以为它合得上。"

Charlie Munger, "A Lesson on Elementary, Worldly Wisdom As It Relates to Investment Management & Business," USC Business School, 1994 (Poor Charlie's Almanack) · 1994[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/usc-1994-worldly-wisdom/)

"我想起了我最喜欢的商业比喻——那只老鼠说：'放我出陷阱吧，我已经决定不要那块奶酪了。'商业陷阱有千千万万种。你可能变得马虎，可能酗酒，可能患上自大狂，可能看不清自己的局限。把事情搞砸的方法有上百万种。"

Janet Lowe, Damn Right! Behind the Scenes with Berkshire Hathaway Billionaire Charlie Munger (Wiley, 2000) · 2000 · as recalled[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/berkshire-hathaway-annual-meetings/)

"你把一个人叫作首席风险官，但他往往实际上扮演的角色，是在你做蠢事时让你自我感觉良好的那个人。"

Berkshire Hathaway annual meeting, 2008 (afternoon session) · 2008[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/berkshire-hathaway-annual-meetings/)

"如果有一种很容易就能消除的无知，你却抱着它不放，那是不光彩的。我不认为那只是一个失误或不够勤勉。我认为让自己比本可以达到的程度还要愚蠢,是不光彩的,这就是我的处世准则。"

Berkshire Hathaway annual meeting, 2015 (afternoon session) · 2015[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/berkshire-hathaway-annual-meetings/)

"现代生活造就了成功的官僚机构,而成功的官僚机构又孕育出失败与愚蠢。怎么可能不是这样呢?这就是现代生活的巨大张力。"

Daily Journal Corporation annual meeting (2019) · 2019[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/daily-journal-2019/)

他称之为"现代生活的巨大悲剧"。

"想想看,如果一次董事会会议是这样开的,那该多么令人耳目一新:大家坐下来,'现在我们要花三个小时,逐一审视我们所有愚蠢的失误,以及我们究竟搞砸了多少。'"

"在工程领域,人们都会留出很大的安全边际。但在金融世界里,人们对安全压根不在乎。他们任由风险膨胀、膨胀、再膨胀。而虚假的会计还在为此推波助澜。"

Berkshire Hathaway annual meeting, 2003 (Whitney Tilson's meeting notes; quoted in Tren Griffin, 'Charlie Munger: The Complete Investor', 2015) · 2003 · as recalled[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/berkshire-hathaway-annual-meetings/)

"人们总对伯克希尔说:'相对于你们的资本,为什么不多承保一些业务呢?别人都在这么干。'……可后来又有人进来问:'为什么去年别人都死了,就你们没事?'也许这两个问题是有关联的。要想最终夺冠,你得先跑完全程。"

Janet Lowe, 'Damn Right!' (Wiley, 2000) · 2000 · as recalled

"聪明而勤奋的人,也无法免于因过度自信而招致的职业灾难。他们往往只是在自己所选择的、更艰险的航程中触礁搁浅——只因他们笃信自己的自我评价,以为自己拥有过人的才华和方法。"

Charlie Munger, 'Investment Practices of Leading Charitable Foundations,' Foundation Financial Officers Group, Santa Monica, October 14, 1998 (Poor Charlie's Almanack) · 1998 · as recalled

"我们一直有足够的明智,在某件事运行得非常好时,坚持把它继续做下去。我会说,我们所展现的,或许可以称为人生的基本算法——重复那些奏效的做法。"

Charlie Munger, "A Lesson on Elementary, Worldly Wisdom As It Relates to Investment Management & Business" (the "Art of Stock Picking" talk), USC Business School, 1994; published in Outstanding Investor Digest, May 5, 1995 · 1994[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/usc-1994-worldly-wisdom/)

Charlie Munger, "A Lesson on Elementary, Worldly Wisdom As It Relates to Investment Management & Business" (the "Art of Stock Picking" talk), USC Business School, 1994; published in Outstanding Investor Digest, May 5, 1995 · 1994[ · where he said it ↗](https://mungerarchive.com/zh/recordings/usc-1994-worldly-wisdom/)
