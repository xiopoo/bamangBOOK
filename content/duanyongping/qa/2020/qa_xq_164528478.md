investing requires only two courses: How to Value a Business, and How to Think About Markets. 
投资只需要两门课程：评估企业价值和正确看待市场。
You don’t have to know how to value all businesses. Start with a small circle of competence, things you can understand. [Look for] things that are selling for less than they’re worth. Forget about things you can’t understand. 
你不必知道评估所有生意。从一个小的能力圈开始，里面是你能理解的生意。找出价格低于价值的生意。忘掉你理解不了的生意。
You need to understand accounting, which has enormous limitations. [You need to] understand when a competitive advantage is durable or fleeting. 
你需要懂会计准则和其中的局限。你需要明白竞争优势是可持续的还是短暂的。
Learn that the market is there to serve you, not instruct you. In the investing business, if you have an IQ of 150, sell 30 points to someone else. You do not need to be a genius. 
学会市场先生是服务你的，而不是指示你的。投资生意，不需要150的智商，把多余的30智商卖给其它人。你不必是天才。
You need to have emotional stability, inner peace and be able to think for yourself, [since] you’re subjected to all sorts of stimuli. 
你必须保持稳定的心理状态、内在的平静和独立思考的能力，因为你会面临各种诱惑。
It’s not a complicated game; you don’t need to understand math. It’s simple, but not easy.
投资不是个复杂的游戏，不需要精通数学。投资很简单但并不容易。
