# Why Buffett doesn't take a performance fee

**原站时间**: 2003-05-03T13:00:00+0000
**会议年份**: 2003

> When Warren Buffett was running his investing partnerships, he got a performance fee, in the form of a big slice of the profits. He explains why he gave that up as head of Berkshire Hathaway.
