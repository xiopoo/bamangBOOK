# Buffett's long-distance rescue bid for Long-Term Capital Management

**原站时间**: 1999-05-03T13:00:00+0000
**会议年份**: 1999

> Warren Buffett discusses the near-collapse of the hedge fund Long-Term Capital Management, which was ultimately bailed out by the Federal Reserve. Warren Buffett describes Berkshire's unsuccessful bid to rescue the firm, which occurred when Buffett and Bill Gates were on a trip together.
