# Buffett: It would be "asinine" for Berkshire to compile a single climate change report

**原站时间**: 2021-05-01T17:30:52+0000
**会议年份**: 2021

> Warren Buffett explains his opposition to a shareholder proposal, backed by institutional investors, calling on Berkshire to compile a climate change report at the corporate level, saying Berkshire Energy and BNSF are the subsidiaries that can have a real impact, and they're already open about the actions they've been taking to reduce their carbon footprint.
