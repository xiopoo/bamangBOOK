# Robinhood takes advantage of "gambling instincts"

**原站时间**: 2021-05-01T19:30:08+0000

> Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger discuss the popular Robinhood trading app, accusing it of encouraging its customers to make short-term bets on stocks and options. Munger calls it "deeply wrong."
