# Buffett discusses David Sokol's resignation

**原站时间**: 2011-04-30T13:00:00+0000

> Berkshire Hathaway executive David Sokol purchased Lubrizol stock ahead of that company's acquisition by Berkshire. Warren Buffett discusses Sokol's "inexplicable" actions and how they violated Berkshire's rules and principles.
