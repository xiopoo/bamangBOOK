# Why Berkshire will need an independent chairman

**原站时间**: 2011-04-30T13:00:00+0000
**会议年份**: 2011

> Warren Buffett wants his son Howard to serve as independent chairman of Berkshire Hathaway after his death. He explains why this is especially important in light of Berkshire executive David Sokol's improper actions and subsequent resignation.
