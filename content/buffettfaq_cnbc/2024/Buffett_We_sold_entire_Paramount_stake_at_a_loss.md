# Buffett: We sold entire Paramount stake at a loss

**原站时间**: 2024-05-06T04:07:04+0000
**会议年份**: 2024

> Warren Buffett reveals that Berkshire Hathaway has sold its entire remaining stake in Paramount Global and "we lost quite a bit of money." He takes 100% responsibility for the position but says the experience has helped him learn more about what people do with their leisure time and how that is affecting entertainment companies.
