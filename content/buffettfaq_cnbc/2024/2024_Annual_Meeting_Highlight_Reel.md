# 2024 Annual Meeting Highlight Reel

**原站时间**: 2024-05-06T04:03:36+0000
**会议年份**: 2024

> Nicknamed "Woodstock for Capitalists," the Berkshire Hathaway annual meeting is a gathering of the company's shareholders presided over by Chairman and CEO Warren Buffett. The Omaha, Nebraska, event gives shareholders a chance to hear Buffett discuss investing, his economic outlook, and life, as well as ask him questions.
