# Jain: Fraud is pushing the price of insurance higher in Florida

**原站时间**: 2024-05-06T04:06:10+0000
**会议年份**: 2024

> Ajit Jain, Berkshire Hathaway's insurance chief, says fraud and severe weather are driving insurance prices higher in Florida. He is, however, optimistic Berkshire will still be able to operate profitably there as the state addresses the fraud problem.
