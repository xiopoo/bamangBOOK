# Buffett and Munger commend DC's credit crisis response

**原站时间**: 2009-05-02T13:00:00+0000
**会议年份**: 2009

> Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger don't entirely agree with everything the government did in response to the 2008 credit crisis, but commend it overall for making tough decisions in extraordinary circumstances.
