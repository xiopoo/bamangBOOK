# Berkshire's Amazon buy isn't a shift away from "value" investing

**原站时间**: 2019-05-04T14:15:14+0000
**会议年份**: 2019

> After Warren Buffett reveals that one of Berkshire Hathaway's two portfolio managers has bought "some" Amazon stock, he rejects the suggestion it's a sign the company is changing its long-standing investment philosophy. He and Charlie Munger, however, have some regrets over a lost tech opportunity for which they are "atoning."
