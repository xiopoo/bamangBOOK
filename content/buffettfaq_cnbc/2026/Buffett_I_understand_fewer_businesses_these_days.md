# Buffett: I 'understand' fewer businesses these days

**原站时间**: 2026-05-04T04:40:16+0000
**会议年份**: 2026

> Warren Buffett tells CNBC's Becky Quick it is a difficult time to find suitable investments for Berkshire, in part because there are fewer businesses he "understands" as technology becomes more complicated. But, he says, it's still possible to find "one that you know makes sense," as Apple did.
