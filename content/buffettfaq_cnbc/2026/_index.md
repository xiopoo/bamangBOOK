# Berkshire Hathaway 年会 2026

(May 2, 2026) In his first shareholders meeting as Berkshire Hathaway's new CEO, Greg Abel stresses that he will maintain the company's long-standing culture, especially its rejection of a centralized bureaucracy. While Warren Buffett sits on the arena floor with his fellow directors, he does speak briefly to praise the board's decision a year before to name Abel as his successor.
