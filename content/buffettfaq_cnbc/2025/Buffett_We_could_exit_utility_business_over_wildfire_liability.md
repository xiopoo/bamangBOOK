# Buffett: We could exit utility business over wildfire liability

**原站时间**: 2025-05-05T05:21:02+0000
**会议年份**: 2025

> Greg Abel describes how Berkshire utilities are working to reduce the risk their equipment could contribute to wildfires, but Warren Buffett warns that if courts and the government unfairly hold them responsible for damages to the extent the businesses are no longer profitable, "a big thing to do is quit."
